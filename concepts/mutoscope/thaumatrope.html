Make a thaumatrope

Assemble your disc

  1. Cut along each circular outline. Cut one matching disc of thin cardboard.
  2. Glue the front drawing to one side of the card, with the small triangle at the top.
  3. Turn the card over from left to right. Glue the back drawing with its triangle also at the top. The artwork on this face is already upside down.
  4. Pierce the two marked side holes and tie a loop of string through each hole. Twist the loops and pull gently to spin the disc.
