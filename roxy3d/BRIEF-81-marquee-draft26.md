## Fixes
1. **Warm light under the canopy.** The soffit downlights should visibly light a soft warm pool on the wet sidewalk
   and the entrance threshold directly below, with broken highlights on the existing wet surfaces and a faint
   response in the entrance glass. Local to the marquee; no change to the surrounding exposure or street lights; no
   blown-out white.
2. **Brighter bulbs.** Warm bright cores with small, restrained halos (glare like the DINK DONK sign's bulbs), a
   little light on their sockets and the trim next to them. Bulbs stay individual dots, never one glowing strip.
3. **More physical letterboard.** Faint horizontal letter tracks, subtle panel seams, gentle backlight variation and
   slightly darker edges inside the frame, on both board faces. Keep it cream, bright and clean (no grime).
4. **Wet roof.** Restrained uneven wet sheen on the canopy top with only plausible reflections of nearby lights.
   Keep it dark.
